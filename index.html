How live scores work

Final scores are pulled automatically from a football data feed on a schedule and shown here; the standings recalculate from completed matches. The bar at the top of the Schedule and Standings tabs always shows the source and when it last updated. Scores are read-only — they come straight from the feed.

About the predictions

The figures next to teams in the standings are for insight and entertainment only — not betting advice or a forecast of any result.

  • # — FIFA World Ranking (official, snapshot 11 Jun 2026): a measure of overall team strength.
  • % — Polymarket: the live "chance to win the group" figure from Polymarket, a prediction market, shown to reflect current market sentiment.
  • 📊 — Match win estimate (on the schedule, upcoming games): a rough chance-to-win derived from the two teams' FIFA ranks — a simple model, not betting odds (it ignores form, injuries and draws).

This site is not affiliated with FIFA or Polymarket, does not accept wagers, and handles no money. If you choose to use a prediction market, do so responsibly and only where it is legal for you.

Sources — where every number comes from

  • Schedule, venues & official results — FIFA (the official source of truth), cross-checked against NBC Sports and FIFA host-city sites (Dallas, NY/NJ, Atlanta).
  • Live scores & standings — football-data.org (free tier). FIFA remains canonical for official results.
  • FIFA World Ranking (#) — the official FIFA/Coca-Cola Men's World Ranking (snapshot 11 Jun 2026).
  • Group odds (%) — Polymarket, a prediction market (live "to win the group" — informational only, not betting advice).
  • Highlights — official broadcaster uploads on YouTube (e.g. FOX Sports); availability varies by region.
  • Live audio (USA) — iHeartRadio (free FOX Sports English commentary).
  • Social buzz — X (#FIFAWorldCup).
  • TV listings (USA) — FOX Sports.
  • Kickoff times — official Eastern kickoff times, converted to your device's timezone.
